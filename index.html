What you could make with Claude

Claude can build printable pages, interactive sites and small tools from a plain description of what you need. A few places to start:

  • Class resources

    • Worksheets and practice sets for this week's skill
    • Vocabulary cards, word walls and sorting activities
    • Reading passages on your topic at your students' level
    • Review games and exit tickets

  • Custom resources

    • The same lesson at two or three reading levels
    • Visual schedules, checklists and step-by-step cards
    • Choice boards and extension work for early finishers
    • Name-based or student-made pages like the name sheets below

  • Teacher tools

    • Calendars and planners that follow the Lausanne rotation
    • Rubric builders and grade conversion charts
    • Random group, partner and seating chart makers
    • Pages that turn an export into something you can print

  • Student sites and activities

    • Interactive practice with instant feedback
    • Simulations, like the pretend grocery store below
    • Research libraries with safe, readable sources
    • Step-by-step lab or project guides

  • Family communication

    • How-to handouts with numbered steps and screenshots
    • Newsletter and weekly update templates
    • Welcome packets and supply lists
    • Event and conference schedules

  • Clubs, teams and events

    • Season and practice plans
    • Slide decks with speaker notes
    • Sign-up sheets and rosters
    • Field trip and event checklists
